Questions first-time visitors ask

Who can use SKILL2EARN?

Students use it to find short, flexible jobs that match their skills. Employers and clients use it to post jobs and hire students.

Which email should I use to sign up?

Students need a school email ending in @tip.edu.ph or @edu.ph. Employers can use any valid email address.

What happens after I sign up?

We send a confirmation link to your email. Open it, log in, then complete your profile. Your dashboard shows the next steps.

Why do I need to verify my account?

Verification shows that students and employers are real, which makes it safer for everyone.
